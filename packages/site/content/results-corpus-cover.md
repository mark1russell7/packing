## Corpus coverage

How much of the problem do the benchmark inputs show? These numbers come from the corpus files in the repository. No input is generated.

- A cell has one value for each of 10 dimensions of the taxonomy. The full product has more than 2.5 million cells.
- A pair is one value of a dimension with one value of another dimension. A pair is covered when an input has both values.
- Each square of the grid is a pair of dimensions, with the percent of its value pairs that are covered.
- A low share of the cells is normal: many cells are not possible, and the count includes them. Read the share as a lower limit.
- A corpus of the benchmark can repeat the cells of another corpus. Use the list to read one corpus alone.
